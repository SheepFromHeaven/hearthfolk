# Getting Started

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.3
- NeoForge 26.3 or newer

Install it on the server and on every player's client. Speech bubbles, the village border outline, the custom villager rendering and the settings screen are drawn on the client.

## The idea

Vanilla already gives villagers beds, workstations, breeding and schedules. Hearthfolk adds behaviour on top of those. If a villager has a bed and a workstation, it works. If it is missing something, it tells you (see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting)).

Villagers also level up by working, not only by trading with you, and their trades are fixed prices you can learn instead of random cycling offers.

## Blocks you place are yours

Any job block, chest or barrel you place yourself is private. Villagers will not claim it, so a workstation you place does nothing until you hand it over. Sneak and right-click it with an emerald in your main hand and villagers may claim it (green sparkles). Do it again to take it back. Blocks the world generated, such as those in a generated village, and the job blocks this mod adds (furnace minecart, nursery, golem workshop, pack station) are claimable straight away. Beds are different: a bed you have slept in is yours, and the emerald does not work on it. Break it to free it.

## Your first village

1. Give each villager a bed and a workstation, as in vanilla, and hand over each workstation you placed (see above).
2. Add a bell. In the late afternoon villagers walk to one and claim it as their meeting point, and a bell that villagers have claimed makes the buildings around it a village.
3. Put a plain chest or a barrel within 8 blocks of each bed and hand it over with an emerald. The villager claims it as its home chest, as long as it has no hopper under it, and locks it for players. Right-click it to see whose it is.
4. Build the workplace for the job (see [Professions](Professions)). A farmer needs a composter beside a field, a miner needs a cobblestone rim and a furnace minecart, and so on.
5. Hand over a tool if the job needs one. Throw it near the villager or trade it over.
6. Wait. A villager with enough emeralds buys breeding food. With a free bed in reach and a willing partner, vanilla breeding does the rest, so the village grows.

## Seeing what is going on

Hold the Villager Inspector (creative tab Tools and Utilities) in your main hand and right-click a villager for a report book. It works on any villager, children and jobless ones too. `/minenow villager` prints a similar report in chat for the nearest working adult villager within 16 blocks. A villager shaking its head, or showing a speech bubble with an item, is telling you what it needs.

## Playing with the settings

Open the mod list, pick Hearthfolk and press Config. In single player this edits the world you are in. See [Settings](Settings).
