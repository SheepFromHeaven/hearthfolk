# Troubleshooting

Start with `/minenow villager` on the villager or the Villager Inspector. It lists what stops it. The reasons, and what they look like in game:

| Reason | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No job site | No job block claimed. It shakes its head, even off duty. | Place a free workstation within reach, or check that you did not mark yours as private. |
| No tool | Its work needs a tool it lacks. It holds up an emerald and walks to you. | Give it the tool, or let a toolsmith or fletcher supply one. |
| No materials | It is out of fuel (butcher, smelter) and nobody sells any. It holds up an emerald. | Give it coal or charcoal, or add a miner. |
| No money | It cannot pay for what it needs. It shakes its head while working. | Throw it emeralds, or add a trade that buys its goods. |
| Storage full | Its pockets and chests are full. It shakes its head. | Add a chest within 6 blocks of its job block, or empty one. |
| No work area | Nothing to work on around its job block (no field, no water, no pen, no mine rim). | Check the layout rules for its job in [Professions](Professions). |

## Common questions

- A villager is not working in the rain: outdoor work only happens on short trips from a roof in rain, so build a roof beside the field, pen or water.
- A villager will not take a job block: the block may be yours (placed by you). Sneak and right-click it with an emerald to let villagers claim it. Also check the profession is not switched off.
- A chest will not open: it is a villager's. Right-click to see whose. Use the Inspector, or turn off Villager Chests Locked.
- My villagers do not trade with each other: they only trade inside one village. Check with the bell report that they share a village.
- A miner did nothing: its rim must be closed cobblestone flush in the ground, with the cart on it.
- A village stopped growing: children need breeding food, a free bed and enough emeralds, which grows with village size. The Breeding page of the Inspector says which is missing.
- I lost items near a villager: use `/minenow free`.
