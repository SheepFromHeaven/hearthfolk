# Professions

Every profession has a trade file (see [Customizing with Data Packs](Customizing-with-Data-Packs)) with what it buys, what it sells and what it crafts. Level 1 is novice, 5 is master.

A workstation or chest you place yourself is yours, and villagers will not claim it until you hand it over. Sneak and right-click it with an emerald in your main hand. Blocks generated with the world, and the job blocks this mod adds (furnace minecart, nursery, golem workshop, pack station), are claimable as they are. A blast furnace or anvil you place yourself is private like any other.

Farmers, fishermen, shepherds and butchers need the tool of their trade, and so do the cartographer (its ink and quill), forester and miner. A villager without one buys it from a crafting villager, picks it up if you throw it, or walks to you holding an emerald. The other vanilla trades need no tool, only materials.

Every profession can be switched off in [Settings](Settings). Farmer through cartographer fall back to vanilla behaviour and vanilla trades. Miner, forester, golemsmith and merchant are Hearthfolk's own, so switched off they lose the job. A smelter at a blast furnace becomes an armorer instead.

## Vanilla professions with real work

### Farmer (composter)
Tends the field beside its composter: harvests ripe crops, replants, tills, bone-meals young crops. The field is the ground touching a side of the composter plus connected farmland and tillable ground within 4 blocks of water. Its reach grows with level (10, 13, 16, 20, 24 blocks). Water, fences, slabs or anything solid on top cut ground out of the field. Pumpkins from journeyman and melons from expert, only from stems. Never tramples farmland. Composts surplus seeds into bone meal. Needs a hoe. Sells crops, seeds, pumpkins and melon slices.

### Fisherman (barrel)
Walks to a shore spot by open water (within 8 blocks of the barrel, 3 up or down), casts a bobber, reels in a bite and keeps the catch in the barrel. Higher levels react faster and wear the rod less. Needs a fishing rod and open water. Sells fish and what it catches.

### Shepherd (loom)
Shears adult sheep in an enclosed pen within 8 blocks of the loom, breeds the flock with wheat (each colour on its own), opens fence gates. Spins wool into string, and weaves carpets from journeyman. Needs shears. Sells wool, string and carpets.

### Butcher (smoker)
Keeps cow, pig, chicken and rabbit herds in an enclosed pen near the smoker: breeds them, culls the surplus (at most 4 animals a day in total, never babies, named or leashed animals), feeds the young and smokes the meat with coal. A pen is a fully fenced area smaller than about 1000 floor cells, and its reach grows with level (8 to 40 blocks). Culling needs an axe, smoking needs fuel and breeding needs feed, and it does whatever it has the supplies for. Sells cooked meat, leather, feathers and rabbit parts.

### Toolsmith (smithing table)
Crafts stone, copper, iron and diamond hoes, axes and pickaxes, fishing rods and shears from materials it buys. Sells them to you and to villagers who lack a tool.

### Fletcher (fletching table)
Crafts sticks, ladders, arrows, wooden tools, bows (journeyman), tripwire hooks and crossbows (expert). Practises archery at a target block within 16 blocks. Buys planks, flint, feathers.

### Armorer (anvil)
Crafts copper armor (novice), shields (apprentice), iron armor (journeyman) and diamond armor (expert). Dresses empty armor stands within 8 blocks of its anvil with its best pieces, never one you dressed. Those stands are locked as its display while it lives, if Villager Chests Locked is on.

### Weaponsmith (grindstone)
Crafts stone, copper, iron and diamond swords, grinds two worn blades into one and hangs its best swords on the wall in item frames.

### Leatherworker (cauldron)
Makes leather, leather armor, item frames, bone meal, dyes, saddles, horse armor and bundles. Bone-meals tall flowers for dye (never picks them), dyes armor, hangs tanning racks and dresses armor stands.

### Librarian (lectern)
Makes paper, books and enchanted books. Books are bound like an enchanting table (power 5 at novice up to 30 at master), and mending or frost walker only from expert. You are offered each book it holds as its own offer with the enchantments shown.

### Cleric (brewing stand)
Brews swiftness (novice), leaping (apprentice), water breathing and healing (journeyman), and strength, poison, harming, night vision and invisibility (expert), plus splash healing, poison and harming. Awkward potions are only a step and are not sold. Heals hurt villagers for a fee and throws splash potions at enemies. Needs water within 8 blocks of the stand and supplies bought from you, other villagers or the travelling merchant.

### Mason (stonecutter)
Cuts slabs, stairs, walls and bricks. From apprentice, while a redstone signal reaches its stonecutter, it replaces the dirt paths within 12 blocks (up to 20 at master) with cobblestone and gravel, and stone bricks from journeyman, using material it holds or buys. A villager with spare money can pay it to pave the path to its bed.

### Cartographer (cartography table)
Draws a map of its village, once the village has a claimed bell (no village, no map), zoomed in with level: roads from apprentice, the border from journeyman, job sites and homes from expert. Its tool is an ink and quill, which it crafts itself.

## Hearthfolk's own professions

### Miner (furnace minecart)
Use a furnace minecart on any block that is not a rail to place the job block. Mark the mine with a closed rim of any cobblestone flush in the ground, with the cart on a rim block. The enclosed cells (within 8 blocks) are the outline. The miner digs a shaft with platforms and tunnels down to a depth that grows with level (8 to 40 blocks, master only stops at bedrock). A redstone signal into the cart limits the depth to that many platforms. It lays ladders and torches, seals water and cave openings, collects ore, coal, cobblestone and gravel, knaps flint from the gravel, and sells what it digs. Needs a pickaxe. A wooden pickaxe leaves iron and copper in the walls, so it buys better ones.

### Forester (nursery)
Put a sapling in a flower pot standing on a log to make the job block. Fells natural trees (trees with natural leaves standing on dirt) within 8 to 16 blocks, cutting from the top down, then replants. Big 2x2 trees only from expert. Gathers saplings, apples and sticks, and turns logs into planks and stripped logs. Needs an axe.

### Smelter (blast furnace)
Smelts ore in its blast furnace and up to four nearby furnaces, charcoal from logs, stone from cobblestone. Buys raw ore from the miner. A villager at a blast furnace is a smelter, one at an anvil is an armorer.

### Golemsmith (golem workshop)
Sneak and right-click an iron block with a carved pumpkin to make the workshop. Mends hurt iron golems with ingots. From journeyman it builds golems slowly, up to a cap by level, at the poppy you planted or beside the workshop. It also sells iron bars, buckets and cauldrons. Villagers no longer summon golems themselves unless that setting is on.

### Merchant (pack station)
Sneak and right-click a hay bale with a saddle to make the pack station. Claims untamed mules nearby (1 at novice, up to 4) and puts chests on them. In the morning it plans a tour to other loaded villages within range that are short of something its village sells, shops for the goods, walks to the bell, holds a market and returns home. At night it makes camp with straw beds. It never trades directly between two villages except through its own market.
