# Commands and the Inspector

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/minenow villager` | Reports the nearest working adult villager within 16 blocks: activity, behaviour running, tool, slots, chests, breeding status, job and the reasons it is idle. Children and jobless villagers are skipped, so use the Villager Inspector on those. |
| `/minenow farmer` | The same for the nearest adult farmer within 16 blocks, with its field. |
| `/minenow free` | Frees every claimed item within 32 blocks of where you stand, so a villager's claim no longer locks things that were yours. |
| `/minenow bubble <item> [seconds]` | Shows a speech bubble with an item over the nearest villager within 8 blocks, for 1 to 600 seconds (default 5). |

Any player can use these commands, no operator rights needed.

## The Villager Inspector

An item in the creative tab Tools and Utilities (creative only, there is no recipe). Hold it in your main hand. Bell and bed reports also work from the off hand. It shows reports on a book screen instead of the chat.

- Right-click a villager: its report, one page per topic (General, Job, Storage, Breeding). Works on any villager, including children and unemployed ones.
- Sneak and right-click a villager: opens its eight slots as a live, editable chest.
- Right-click a villager's chest or barrel: opens it even though it is locked, and says whose it is.
- Right-click a job block or bed: who claimed it, or that nobody has.
- Right-click a bell: the village it belongs to, its loaded villagers, the number of free beds and each free job block (yours never count as free). It never rings the bell.
- Left-click a bell: toggle the village border outline (lime) and where it would grow (orange).
