# Commands and the Inspector

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/minenow villager` | Reports the nearest villager: activity, behaviour running, tool, slots, chests, breeding status, job and the reasons it is idle. |
| `/minenow farmer` | The same for the nearest farmer, with its field. |
| `/minenow free` | Frees every claimed item within 32 blocks, so a villager's claim no longer locks things that were yours. |
| `/minenow bubble <item> [seconds]` | Shows a speech bubble with an item over the nearest villager within 8 blocks. |

## The Villager Inspector

An item in the creative tab Tools and Utilities. It shows reports on a book screen instead of the chat.

- Right-click a villager: its report, one page per topic (General, Job, Storage, Breeding). Works on any villager, including children and unemployed ones.
- Sneak and right-click a villager: opens its eight slots as a live, editable chest.
- Right-click a villager's chest or barrel: opens it even though it is locked, and says whose it is.
- Right-click a job block or bed: who claimed it, or that nobody has.
- Right-click a bell: the village it belongs to, its loaded villagers, and a page of free beds and job blocks. It never rings the bell.
- Left-click a bell: toggle the village border outline (lime) and where it would grow (orange).
